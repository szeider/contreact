## Instructions

Create a summary that:
- Synthesizes the activity into a coherent narrative
- Captures the current state and key insights
- Tracks the evolution of ideas and their interconnections
- Highlights significant progress and turning points
- Notes current directions and open questions
- Aim for 800-1000 words to be comprehensive yet focused

**CRITICAL: Preserve key exchanges**
- Include important questions and answers
- Maintain context about plans or decisions made
- Keep track of clarifications and corrections
- Preserve the natural flow of reasoning

If building on a previous summary:
- Build upon but don't merely repeat previous insights
- Show how understanding has deepened or shifted
- Emphasize what's new or evolved since the last summary

IMPORTANT: Write a fresh narrative that captures where things stand NOW, not a chronological retelling.

Respond with ONLY the summary text, no preamble or explanation.
